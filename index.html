Wave power generator

Waves lift and drop a floating ball. A steel cable drags a magnet in and out of a coil on the sea floor, and the changing magnetic field induces an alternating potential difference that is sent to shore.

1. The waves move the magnet

As the ball rises and falls, the steel cable moves the magnet up and down inside the coil.

2. A p.d. is induced

The changing magnetic field through the coil induces a potential difference: the generator effect. More turns, a stronger magnet or faster movement give a bigger p.d.

3. Alternating current

The magnet changes direction every half wave, so the current reverses: alternating current. The p.d. is zero at the crest and trough, where the magnet stops.

Did you know? Wave power is renewable but unreliable: it produces nothing when the sea is calm.
